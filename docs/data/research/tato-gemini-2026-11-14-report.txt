Georgia Tech Campus Map Inventory for Fall 2026

This inventory documents 101 sourced campus and near-campus places for an interactive Georgia Tech map as of November 14, 2026 (Fall 2026), built strictly from institutional pages (gatech.edu subsystems) alongside official partner/facility sites (bmemakerspaces.com, codatechsquare.com). Category counts across the 101 features are: food (10), support (14), printing (13), makerspace (6), exercise (5), health (5), transport (21), and extra (27).   

Within the 101 sourced entries, 21 carry high confidence because their official sources are explicitly dated for Fall 2026: Tech Dining pages showing November 22–28, 2026 Thanksgiving hours, Campus Recreation Center (CRC) and Ken Byers Tennis Complex Fall 2026 schedules, Student Center daily operations (Nov. 2026 / Dec. 2026 schedules), Parking and Transportation Services (PTS) bike-room updates modified October 14, 2026, CoC TSO IT Support, Flowers Invention Studio and The Hive Fall 2026 schedules, and the Center for Mental Health Care and Resources (CMHCR) supported by Fall 2026 syllabi and group listings. The remaining 80 entries carry medium confidence because they rely on undated official pages (Georgia Tech Library computing and study-space guides, EOC Title IX lactation listings, Stamps Health Services hours, OIT/student-service desks) or older-term official records—specifically Stinger route announcements from Fall 2025 and Spring 2026, an embedded College of Sciences clinician schedule from Fall 2025–Spring 2026, and Bursar/Financial Aid advising hours from a Fall 2019 orientation packet.   

Several operational shifts and inventory gaps affect completeness. First, no retrieved source explicitly states geographic coordinates for individual campus buildings or entrances inside the -84.42 to -84.375 longitude and 33.76 to 33.79 latitude bounding box; therefore, geometry and coord_source are null across all 101 GeoJSON features rather than estimated. Second, student printing runs on OIT/ECE/CoC Pharos and Central-PS systems rather than PaperCut; CARE and the Counseling Center consolidated into CMHCR; Pharmabox relocated to Clough Commons 3rd floor; and PTS updated its Stinger shuttle network in October 2025 (moving the Blue Route westbound stop from the Weber turnaround to the Ferst Drive shelter and listing seven weekday routes without a separate Trolley route, though undated OIT directions still reference the Yellow Express/Trolley). Major missing items include North Ave Dining Hall hours/address, Wreck Stop, Wreck Techs, regular Housing Area Office hours, the Registrar's building location, Relay bike share stations, outdoor athletic fields (Roe Stamps / SAC Fields), and verified point locations for all-gender restrooms, water-refill stations, and accessible entrances (detailed in Section 3).
